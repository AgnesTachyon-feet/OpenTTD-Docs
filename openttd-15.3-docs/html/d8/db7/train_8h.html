Modes for ignoring signals.

Enumerator
TFP_NONE 

Normal operation.

TFP_STUCK 

Proceed till next signal, but ignore being stuck till then. This includes force leaving depots.

TFP_SIGNAL 

Ignore next signal, after the signal ignore being stuck.
